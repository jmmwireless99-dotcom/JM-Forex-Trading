PAANO MALALAMAN BUY O SELL
--------------------------
1) TOP-RIGHT yellow/lime/red badge:

   NOW: WAIT  ...     = HUWAG mag-entry
   NOW: WATCH BUY     = pwede maghanap ng BUY (hintay closed candle)
   NOW: WATCH SELL    = pwede maghanap ng SELL

2) Sa TAAS ng candle (hindi sa gold line):

   lime  ^ BUY   = ENTRY BUY  (oras OK)
   red   v SELL  = ENTRY SELL (oras OK)
   yellow ^ BUY  = BUY setup, V640 hour OFF
   orange v SELL = SELL setup, V640 hour OFF

Gold "BIAS BUY" = trend lang. HINDI yun ang entry.

Sa screenshot v1.32: dots=0 at V640 both OFF
  = walang entry. v1.33 may NOW badge + mas kitang dots.
